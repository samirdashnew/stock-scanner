Scanner Backtest

Mechanical backtest of the exact opening-range/VWAP/RVOL rules the live scanner uses
Every qualifying setup in the window below became one simulated trade: stop-loss and target checked bar-by-bar, unresolved trades closed at end of day. This is a mechanical test of the rule, not a live track record — real trading adds slippage, the free-data lag noted on Morning Picks, and your own judgment on which setups to actually take. Past performance, simulated or real, does not predict future results. Sample sizes here are still small (see trade counts below) — treat these numbers as directional, not final, and re-run periodically as more data accumulates.
Is the displayed target realistic? Not usually, and that's worth being clear about: most winning trades close positive by end of day well short of the stated target — see "Wins that hit the actual target" below. Treat the target as a stretch level to trim into, not an expected exit. The win/loss classification (and the expectancy number) already account for this correctly either way — it's the specific target price that's rarely the real exit.
