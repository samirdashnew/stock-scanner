NSE F&O Momentum Scanner

Volume-surge + price-momentum ranking, refreshed by scanner.py
→ Morning Picks (buy/sell shortlist, run after 9:30 AM)
RVOL = today's volume ÷ 20-day average volume (relative volume — the core liquidity/interest signal).
Score = weighted blend of RVOL, % price move, and how close the close is to the day's high/low (conviction).
Turnover = 20-day avg price × volume, in ₹ crore (liquidity filter).
