Morning Picks

Opening-range breakout + volume + VWAP shortlist — run once after 9:30 AM IST
Before you act on this: free data can lag 15-20 minutes — check the live price on your broker terminal before entering. This is a screening shortlist, not a signal that guarantees a profitable trade; position size and manage risk accordingly. Stop-loss levels shown are structural (opening-range level), not a recommendation on how much capital to risk. SELL picks are intraday short-sell setups — in the NSE cash segment these must be squared off the same day (no overnight short without F&O); confirm your broker/segment supports intraday short-selling before acting on one.
RVOL (early) = volume so far today, scaled to a full trading day, vs the 20-day average — the volume-conviction signal.
Target = entry ± 2 × (entry − stop), a 2:1 reward:risk level, not a price prediction.
BUY: broke above the first-15-min high, trading above VWAP, early RVOL ≥ 1.5x, stop within ~1.5% of entry. SELL: mirror image — broke below the first-15-min low, trading below VWAP, same volume/stop conditions.
