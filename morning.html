How does this scanner decide? ▾
RVOL = volume so far today, scaled to a full trading day, vs the 20-day average.
Target = entry ± reward-multiple × (entry − stop) — a reward:risk level, not a price prediction.
BUY: broke above the first-15-min high, trading above VWAP, early RVOL ≥ 1.5x, stop within ~1.5% of entry, trading with the daily trend (above EMA50) with a strong trend (ADX14 ≥ 20) and RSI not already exhausted. SELL: full mirror image.
